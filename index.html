How to measure

Measure your wrist

  1. Wrap a soft tape measure, or a strip of paper, around your wrist just above the bone.
  2. Mark where it meets, then measure that length in inches.
  3. Enter it in the size finder above and choose your fit.
Care

Keep the shine

  • Put it on last. Lotion, perfume and hairspray can dull pearls and porous stones like turquoise.
  • Take it off for water. Swimming, showers and dishes weaken stringing wire and cord over time.
  • Store pieces apart. Harder stones can scratch softer ones, so keep each piece in its own pouch.
  • Wipe, don't soak. A soft dry cloth after wearing is all most pieces need.
